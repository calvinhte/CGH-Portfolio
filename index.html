MyHakbang - Capstone Project

MyHakbang is an AI-assisted, mobile-compliant web-based appointment and records management system developed for Hakbang Center for Child Development. The system was designed to centralize appointment scheduling, patient records management, and administrative operations into a single digital platform, improving the efficiency and accessibility of clinic services. The platform allows patients and parents to conveniently book appointments online through a web-based and mobile-friendly interface. It also integrates artificial intelligence features such as chatbot-assisted inquiries and Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) support for therapists, helping streamline communication, assist with preliminary assessments, and support patient progress tracking.

My Role: I served as the Communications and Documentation Lead. This role involved coordinating communication between team members, stakeholders, and clients to ensure smooth project execution. I was responsible for documenting project requirements, manuscript, development processes, meetings and testing outcomes. Beyond my primary role, I was involved in the technical implementation. I specifically coded and designed the AI Chatbot using the Dialogflow API with Tiered Response System (TRS). It is an AI-assisted chatbot mechanism that classifies and handles user inquiries across multiple response levels. It begins with automated responses for common queries and progressively escalates complex or concerns to admin personnel.

Attendify

Attendify is a software development project designed to digitalize faculty attendance tracking across the campus. The system allows designated checkers or personnel to monitor and verify professors’ attendance through a more efficient and organized digital process.

Instead of using traditional paper-based attendance signing, Attendify utilizes image-based verification by capturing classroom photos with the professor present during scheduled class hours. This helps confirm whether professors are present or absent while reducing manual paperwork and improving the accuracy of attendance monitoring.

My Role: We the KDC group, four member group created this project. I was responsible in assisting the Backend functionality of this project and helped fix the user interface as well.

Coffee US Cafe – Business Information Website

Coffee US Cafe is a small outdoor coffee shop business owned and managed by a couple. The café offers a wide variety of beverages and creates a cozy and welcoming environment for customers. In addition to their regular operations, they also participate in small pop-up events and local gatherings.

This was a two-person project focused on creating an informational website that showcases the café’s products, brand identity, and contact information. My contributions included designing the website interface, developing the front-end components, and assisting with the project documentation. The website was created to help strengthen the café’s online presence and make information more accessible to customers.
